Athletic Lounge Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Athletic Lounge is a workout tracking app. This policy explains what data the app stores, why it is used, and how users can request account and cloud-data deletion.

Contact: pinto.gael310@gmail.com

Data Athletic Lounge stores

The app may store workout logs, set metrics, routines, custom exercises, workout photos, body measurements, heart-rate workout samples, local heart-rate trend summaries, app settings, account profile fields, Pro entitlement status, and diagnostics needed to keep the app reliable.

Local-first tracking

Workout tracking is designed to work offline. Training data is stored on the user's device first. Cloud backup, restore, and auto-sync are used only when a user signs in and uses account sync features.

Cloud account data

Signed-in account data is stored in Supabase under the user's account. Current Pro cloud backup covers profile and app settings, equipment, body measurements, workout history, sets, heart-rate samples, ratings and notes, personal records, and recovery events. Custom exercises, workouts, routines, current or active workout state, and workout photos remain local to the device and are not currently included in cloud backup.

Ads and purchases

The current closed-testing build does not show or request ads. It also disables new purchase, purchase restore, and subscription-management controls. Existing Pro status may still be read from the signed-in account. Athletic Lounge does not directly process payment card details.

Health and fitness data

Heart-rate and body-measurement features are for training feedback only. Athletic Lounge is not a medical device and should not be used for medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical decisions.

How data is used

Data is used to provide workout tracking, recovery and progression feedback, app settings, account sync, Pro entitlement, support, and reliability diagnostics. Athletic Lounge does not sell personal training data.

Account and data deletion

Users can request deletion from inside the app at Settings > Privacy & Data > Request account deletion, or by using the public instructions on the account deletion page:

Athletic Lounge account deletion instructions

Deletion requests should include the signed-in email address and user id shown in the app when available. Local device data can also be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from Android settings.

Children

Athletic Lounge is not directed to children. Users should not create an account or submit data if they are not old enough to use fitness tracking services in their location.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Athletic Lounge changes. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes materially.
