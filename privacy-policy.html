Athletic Lounge Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Athletic Lounge is a workout tracking app. This policy explains what data the app stores, why it is used, and how users can request account and cloud-data deletion.

Contact: pinto.gael310@gmail.com

Data Athletic Lounge stores

The app may store workout logs, set metrics, routines, custom exercises, workout photos, body measurements, heart-rate workout samples, local heart-rate trend summaries, app settings, account profile fields, Pro entitlement status, and diagnostics needed to keep the app reliable.

Local-first tracking

Workout tracking is designed to work offline. Training data is stored on the user's device first. Cloud backup, restore, and auto-sync are used only when a user signs in and uses account sync features.

Cloud account data

Signed-in account data is stored in Supabase under the user's account. Current Pro cloud backup covers profile and app settings, equipment, body measurements, workout history, sets, heart-rate samples, ratings and notes, personal records, and recovery events. Custom exercises, workouts, routines, current or active workout state, and workout photos remain local to the device and are not currently included in cloud backup.

Ads and purchases

Free users may receive Google AdMob ads after Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow permits ad requests. Where required, users can review their ad privacy choices in Settings > Privacy & Data. Pro removes ads and is purchased, restored, and managed through Google Play. Athletic Lounge receives purchase verification and entitlement information, but does not directly process payment card details.

Google Mobile Ads may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising and other device or account identifiers, app/ad interactions, and SDK performance diagnostics for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. This data is encrypted in transit. Collection depends on consent, device settings, and Google's ad-serving behavior. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Authentication and security

Account authentication is provided by Supabase. When users choose Google sign-in, Google authenticates them and supplies basic account information, such as email and profile details, to Supabase for account access. Administrative credentials and Google Play verification secrets remain on the server and are not included in the app.

Athletic Lounge's own troubleshooting logs remain in private device storage and are not automatically uploaded. Google's advertising SDK diagnostics are handled separately as described above.

Health and fitness data

Heart-rate and body-measurement features are for training feedback only. Athletic Lounge is not a medical device and should not be used for medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical decisions.

How data is used

Data is used to provide workout tracking, recovery and progression feedback, app settings, account sync, Pro entitlement, support, and reliability diagnostics. Athletic Lounge does not sell personal training data.

Account and data deletion

Signed-in users can permanently delete their account and associated Supabase cloud data at Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete account and cloud data, after confirming twice. Users unable to sign in can use the public instructions on the account deletion page:

Athletic Lounge account deletion instructions

Support deletion requests should include the account email and user id when available; ownership is verified before deletion. Cloud-account deletion does not remove files stored only on the device. Local data can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. Account deletion does not cancel a Google Play subscription; manage subscriptions separately in Google Play.

Children

Athletic Lounge is not directed to children. Users should not create an account or submit data if they are not old enough to use fitness tracking services in their location.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Athletic Lounge changes. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes materially.
