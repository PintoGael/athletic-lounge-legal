Athletic Lounge Account Deletion

Request deletion of your Athletic Lounge account and associated app-controlled cloud and device data. You can submit the request from the app or by email.

Request deletion in the app

  1. Open Athletic Lounge.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Open Privacy & Data.
  4. Tap Request account deletion.
  5. Review and send the prepared support email.

The email includes your account identifiers to help support verify ownership. Sending the email submits a request; wait for confirmation that deletion has completed. Never send your password, verification codes or Google credentials.

If you cannot access the app

Email pinto.gael310@gmail.com with the subject "Athletic Lounge account deletion request".

Include the signed-in email address and user id shown in Settings > Privacy & Data when available.

Data deleted

Deletion requests cover all app-controlled data associated with the verified account, including authentication and profile records, settings and equipment, custom exercise/workout/routine definitions, workout history and sets, heart-rate samples and trends, ratings and notes, personal records, body measurements, recovery events, active-workout and review drafts, photos, account-workspace snapshots, diagnostics, and app-managed purchase verification and entitlement records.

Data retained

The developer's policy is to remove all app-controlled data under the verified account when completing deletion. Google Play's own purchase records are controlled separately by Google. Deleting an Athletic Lounge account does not cancel a Google Play subscription or issue a refund; manage subscriptions separately in Google Play.

Local device data

Support coordinates the device-data steps needed for the verified account. Keep app data available while transfer or recovery is pending. Do not clear storage during pending recovery or remove another account's files.

Processing time

Requests require account-ownership verification and confirmation of completion. The app's request button opens an email and does not report completed deletion.
