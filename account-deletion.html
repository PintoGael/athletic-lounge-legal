Athletic Lounge Account Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of an Athletic Lounge account and related cloud data.

Request deletion in the app

  1. Open Athletic Lounge.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Open Privacy & Data.
  4. Tap Request account deletion.
  5. Send the prepared email from your email app.

Request deletion by email

Email pinto.gael310@gmail.com with the subject "Athletic Lounge account deletion request".

Include the signed-in email address and user id shown in Settings > Privacy & Data when available.

Data deleted

Account deletion removes the Athletic Lounge cloud account data tied to the requested account, including account profile fields, settings and equipment, cloud workout-history backups, sets, heart-rate samples, workout ratings and notes, personal records, body measurements, recovery events, and Pro entitlement records managed by Athletic Lounge. Custom exercises, workouts, routines, active workout state, and workout photos are currently local-only and are removed separately with local app data.

Data retained

Some records may be retained only when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, purchase reconciliation, or platform reporting. Google Play purchase records are managed by Google.

Local device data

Deleting cloud account data does not automatically erase local files already stored on a phone. Local Athletic Lounge data can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from Android settings.

Processing time

Deletion requests are reviewed after account ownership can be identified and are completed within 30 days unless a legal requirement permits or requires specific retention.
